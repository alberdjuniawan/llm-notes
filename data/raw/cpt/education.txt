Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy. This process mainly takes place in the leaves, where chlorophyll absorbs light energy. During photosynthesis, plants use carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

The solar system consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it. These objects include planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, and comets. The planets have different sizes, compositions, and distances from the Sun.

A fraction represents a part of a whole. It consists of a numerator and a denominator. The numerator indicates how many parts are being considered, while the denominator indicates how many equal parts make up the whole.
